## Implemented behavior

1. **Interactive Scroll-Driven 3D Evidence Observatory (`EvidenceScene3D.tsx`):**
   - Procedural crystalline faceted core (Icosahedron) in metallic silver and gold materials.
   - Dual concentric orbital rings representing multi-source literature integration (PubMed and Europe PMC).
   - 4 jewel-toned PICO entity satellite beacons (Population, Intervention, Comparison, Outcome).
   - Camera tracking and geometric convergence interpolated seamlessly across scroll progress using `THREE.MathUtils.lerp` in `useFrame`.
   - Strictly lazy-loaded via `React.lazy` with instant fallback to animated SVG `HeroStatic` for no-WebGL / reduced-motion environments.

2. **Ultra-Luxe Apple & Lusion-Grade Design System (`index.css`):**
   - Polished silver (`#e2e8f0`, `#cbd5e1`), gold champagne (`#d4af37`, `#fbbf24`), obsidian black (`#050811`), and pure studio white (`#f8fafc`).
   - Frosted glass cards (`glass-panel-luxury`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and specular 1px border lines.
   - Comprehensive Light and Dark theme modes with instant toggle and `localStorage` persistence.

3. **Refined Landing Page & Header Navigation (`LandingPage.tsx`, `Header.tsx`):**
   - Apple-style typography and feature showcase pillars for Multi-Source Ingestion, Deduplication, and PICO Tagging.
   - Interactive theme switch button with accessible aria labels in the persistent header.

4. **Code-Splitting Verification:**
