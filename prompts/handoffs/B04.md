## Implemented behavior

1. **Cross-Version Group Regression Test Suite (`backend/tests/level2/test_group_regression.py`):**
   - **Bridge Records Isolation:** Validated that introducing a bridge record (linking previously separate groups A and B) only modifies the newly created revision snapshot. Restoring the prior snapshot returns the exact original partition and representatives.
   - **Cannot-Link & Rejected Edge Invariants:** Proved that human negative overrides (`confirm=False`) survive subsequent search query runs, preventing erroneous transitive unification even if fuzzy similarity scores would otherwise suggest duplicate linkage.
   - **Separate Publications from One Trial:** Verified that trial protocols, primary results, and 5-year follow-up publications sharing clinical trial registries (e.g. NCT identifiers) remain distinct paper groups across search revisions unless explicit publication-level identity is proven.
   - **Split Lineage & Ambiguity Handling:** Tested splitting a merged group into separate child groups across revisions: confirmed that provenance is recorded (`GroupLineageOp.SPLIT`), prior merged screening decisions are held in review ambiguity (`ConflictKind.SPLIT_AMBIGUITY`), and PICO annotations stay tied to the historical text without silent propagation.
   - **Conflicting Decision Protection:** Proved that when child groups inherit conflicting human decisions (e.g., one parent Included, another parent Excluded), an active conflict is flagged, effective decision state becomes `CONFLICT`, and the group is strictly barred from export until explicitly resolved.
   - **Deterministic Partition & Representative Choice:** Demonstrated that group clustering order and representative assignment are strictly invariant to input record insertion order or arrival timing.

   - Ensures that when a reviewer resolves a conflict, the chosen human decision immediately takes effect across the review system and allows valid exports.
